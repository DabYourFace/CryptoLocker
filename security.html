Security by Design

CryptoLocker is a non-custodial asset attestation platform. We verify and attest to digital asset ownership and state without holding funds, controlling wallets, or accessing private keys. This architectural separation is the foundation of our security model.

Bank-Level Encryption

  • All sensitive data encrypted using AES-256
  • Encryption applied at rest and in transit
  • Protects credentials, metadata, and attestations
Learn More

Two-Factor Authentication

  • Email or authenticator-based verification
  • Prevents unauthorized account access
  • Strong defense against credential compromise
Learn More

Non-Custodial Architecture

  • Private keys are never collected or stored
  • Wallet connections are read-only
  • Eliminates custodial and counterparty risk

Audits & Compliance Mindset

  • Security practices aligned with industry standards
  • Designed to support attestations and disclosures
  • Built for transparency, not trust-me promises
