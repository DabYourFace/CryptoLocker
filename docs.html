CryptoLocker Documentation

Attestation Mission Statement: CryptoLocker operates under a non-custodial attestation model. This means we provide a platform for you to centralize, verify, and monitor your crypto wallets without ever taking custody of your funds. Attestation allows us to confirm your ownership and activity without exposing private keys, offering you legal clarity, accountability, and secure asset management. By using CryptoLocker, you are benefiting from this verification framework while maintaining full control over your assets.
