Frequently Asked Questions

What is CryptoLocker?

CryptoLocker is a non-custodial digital asset isolation and attestation system. We do not hold, manage, move, or access your assets. Instead, we provide cryptographic proof, structural separation, and verifiable records that establish ownership, control boundaries, and operational intent without custody.

Why does CryptoLocker exist?

Traditional custodial platforms expose users to counterparty risk, asset seizure, mismanagement, insolvency, and legal ambiguity. CryptoLocker was designed to eliminate these risks by ensuring assets remain fully under user control while still providing provable structure, documentation, and separation for legal, financial, and organizational purposes.

How does CryptoLocker work?

CryptoLocker operates through cryptographic attestation, wallet segregation, and structured metadata. Users establish wallets or asset containers that are logically isolated and documented without transferring custody.

  • No private keys are ever collected or stored
  • No transaction authority is granted to CryptoLocker
  • Assets remain on-chain under user-controlled wallets
  • Attestation records prove structure, not possession

What does “non-custodial” actually mean?

Non-custodial means CryptoLocker has zero technical ability to access, freeze, move, or recover your assets. This aligns with established legal definitions used in digital asset regulation, including guidance from FinCEN, SEC custody rules, and international digital asset frameworks.

How does this provide legal protection?

CryptoLocker creates clear separation of ownership, control, and operational responsibility. This separation helps reduce exposure to asset commingling, third-party liability, and custodial classification.

By maintaining user-controlled custody and documented attestation, CryptoLocker supports legal clarity in areas such as:

  • Asset ownership disputes
  • Operational segregation
  • Compliance documentation
  • Counterparty risk mitigation

Is CryptoLocker anonymous?

CryptoLocker supports both identified and anonymous usage models. Users may choose identity-linked verification or fully anonymous wallet-based interaction, depending on their jurisdiction, risk profile, and use case.

Does CryptoLocker comply with regulations?

CryptoLocker is designed to operate within existing legal frameworks by avoiding custody, brokerage, or asset management roles. This architectural choice reduces regulatory exposure while still enabling lawful use, documentation, and proof.

What happens if CryptoLocker disappears?

Nothing happens to your assets. They remain exactly where they are, under your control, on-chain. CryptoLocker cannot lock, revoke, or interfere with access because it never had access in the first place.

Who is CryptoLocker for?

  • Individuals seeking asset isolation without custody risk
  • Organizations needing verifiable asset segregation
  • Users concerned with sovereignty and control
  • Anyone tired of trusting third parties with irreversible assets
